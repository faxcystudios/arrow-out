Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. About Arrow Out

Arrow Out is a puzzle game developed and published by Faxcy Studios.

2. Information We Collect

Arrow Out does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address in order to play the game.

Gameplay information such as progress, settings, coins, stars, and other game-related data may be stored locally on your device.

3. Advertising

Arrow Out uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process certain information from your device for purposes such as delivering advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud, and maintaining the security of advertising services.

Advertising services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Analytics

Faxcy Studios does not intentionally use a separate analytics service to track your gameplay activity.

5. Data Sharing

Faxcy Studios does not sell your personal information. Information processed by third-party services, such as Google AdMob, is handled according to those services' applicable privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay data remains on your device until it is removed by the user, the app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Because Arrow Out does not require an account, Faxcy Studios does not maintain a user account containing your gameplay profile.

7. Children's Privacy

Arrow Out is not specifically directed to children as its target audience. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information directly from children through an account or registration system.

8. Third-Party Services

Arrow Out may use third-party services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Arrow Out, its advertising services, or applicable requirements change.

The updated policy will be made available through the app and this published Privacy Policy webpage.
